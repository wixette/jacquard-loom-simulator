# Image attribution

Reference photos, historical engravings and public-domain book plates for the Jacquard loom research. Each file keeps the licence of its source; none is NC or ND. Files from Wikimedia Commons keep their Commons filenames (spaces as underscores); Flickr files are prefixed `Flickr_<user>_<photo id>_`. Commons images larger than 1920 px were downloaded as Commons' standard 1920 px thumbnail (the Commons API no longer serves arbitrary widths such as 2000 px), and large files were then scaled to at most 1920 px on the long side and re-encoded as JPEG (quality 82); `Jacquard_1810.png` was converted to `Jacquard_1810.jpg` for size. Flickr photos were downloaded at 2048 px and scaled to 1920 px. Book plates in `pd-scans/` are page images from the Internet Archive, at most 2000 px wide, named `<Author>_<year>_p<page>_fig<figures>_<subject>.jpg`. Nothing else was changed. Full details, including what each image shows, are in `../data/photo_catalog.json`.
