# M10 notes: the tutorial's part 6, what came next

- **Status:** built 2026-10-28, on the branch `release/v0.2.0`, the last milestone of release 0.2.0 ([public-app.md](public-app.md) §4). The steps pass `tests/teach/tutorial.test.js` and M7's L-1 on the app.

## The steps

| Step | What it says |
|---|---|
| `next-babbage` | Babbage chose Jacquard's cards for the Analytical Engine in 1836: operation cards and variable cards (the card cylinder, U2, on the stage) |
| `next-lovelace` | Lovelace's notes of 1843: "weaves algebraical patterns…" (Note A), and cards "backed" to repeat (Note C) |
| `next-hollerith` | The census of 1890: cards that hold data; the conductor's ticket punch, by Hollerith's own account |
| `next-three-cards` | Three kinds of card; which held data (a question) |
| `next-computer` | What a computer adds: choice, arithmetic, writing back, the program in its memory; Go to Weave |

## Decisions

1. **The history follows the sources, not the legend** (research bibliography: LOVELACE-1843, BABBAGE-1864, BABBAGE-1836, HOLLERITH). The year 1836 without a day, since the exact date is not confirmed. Hollerith is not drawn as a direct line from Jacquard: he credited a conductor punching tickets, did not say the loom inspired him, and did not know Babbage's work.
2. **No pictures:** the research's freely licensed set has none of Babbage's or Hollerith's machines; the 3D card cylinder and the whole loom stand in.
3. **The part ends where the app began:** in Weave, the learner's own loom.
