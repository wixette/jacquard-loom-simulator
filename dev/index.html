Developer pages

One page per milestone of the Jacquard loom simulator, each kept working as the next is built.

  • M0 · One cross-row in 2D: punch eight-hole cards, then step or play the pick cycle in a side section of the head, drawn to scale from Posselt (1887), and watch the cloth grow as 0s and 1s.
  • M1 · The whole loom in 2D: a head of 8 to 64 hooks, the chain with the real card on the bench, the loom from the side and the front, the woven cloth in colour, the trace of any thread back to its hole, and links.
  • M2 · The learner page: the picture helper (draw, open an image or take a preset; read it directly or as damask; see the floats), lessons L1–L7, and the learner page that brings them to the whole loom.
  • M3 · The core units in 3D: the selector, the card cylinder, the griffe and lift, the harness line, and the shed with its shuttle and batten, one of each part at its documented size, moving with the loom beside the 2D section.
  • M4 · The ghosted whole loom: the loom's frame and head, translucent, with the five core units in place and bands counting what the real machine repeats; click a unit to open it. The learner page has the same view under Parts in 3D.
  • The name game (prototype): type a name; it is set in a pixel font, cut into cards and woven as real damask on the 400 head (or quickly, pixel for pixel), then read back from the cloth. The first of the public app's four mini games (design).
