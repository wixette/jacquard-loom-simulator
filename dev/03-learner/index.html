M2 · The learner page

M2's page is the learner page itself, at the machine's root: the whole loom in 2D with one dock for the lessons, the picture helper and "about this machine". With ?selftest it runs the checks of every milestone (M0 to M4) and the label checks (notes).

  • The learner page with ?selftest

Each lesson

  1. One card, one row
  2. Plain weave
  3. Twill
  4. Satin
  5. A picture
  6. Your picture (the picture helper)
  7. Loops and repeats
  8. Debug the deck (M4)
  9. A program, but not a computer (M4)
  10. Make a real card (M4: prints the cards at true size)

The learner page with the parts in 3D (M4).
