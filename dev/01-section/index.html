    The cloth

    First pick at the bottom. 1 = the warp end lies over the weft; 0 = the weft lies over it. A row joins the cloth when the batten beats it up.

    Simplifications: the cards' timing within a pick is estimated (the order of events is documented); a pushed hook is drawn moving sideways above the grate (the real wire flexes); the cylinder's swing is drawn as a slide; the harness is shortened and hangs straight down; stepping back unweaves exactly.
